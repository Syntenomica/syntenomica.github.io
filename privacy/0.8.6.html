Syntenomica Privacy Policy

Syntenomica is a microscopy research app. It processes selected images and videos on your phone. This policy describes the current free version, what stays on your device, and what happens when you choose to share a file.

1Who we are

Syntenomica is developed by Dániel Sági, an independent developer based in Hungary. For personal data that we receive directly, such as an email or a contribution you choose to send, the developer is the data controller. Contact: syntenomica@gmail.com.

2Data on your device

The app stores private copies of the microscope images and videos you select, projects, sample information, instrument and objective profiles, notes, calibration settings, file fingerprints, previews, and computed results in its private storage on your device. These may include focus composites, relief maps, measurements and identification results. The app does not automatically upload these files to a developer server.

Automatic Android backup and device-to-device transfer of the app's private data are disabled. Original recordings remain in the location from which you selected them.

3Processing and permissions

Image and video processing, including focus stacking, relief computation and the experimental organism identification model, runs on your device. The model is bundled with the app.

You select files through Android's system file picker. The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts or location access. The current free version has Premium and daily import limits turned off. Although the Google Play Billing library is included in the app package, this version does not call its connection, product-query or purchase-query APIs during normal use. Paid features are not available in this release.

4Exporting and sharing

You start an export or share action and choose where it goes using Android's file picker or share sheet. Depending on the action, exported files can contain images or videos, project and sample information, calibration settings, measurements and processing metadata. The receiving app, service or person handles the copy under its own terms. Uninstalling Syntenomica does not delete copies you have exported or shared.

Temporary files created for sharing are kept in the app's private cache and are cleared by the app or Android over time.

5Image contributions

The optional identification contribution feature prepares a ZIP containing the image you selected, the label you provide, the model's displayed guesses, and technical details such as the file name, dimensions, SHA-256 fingerprint, app version, model version and package creation time. The app then opens Android's share sheet. Nothing is sent automatically: you choose the receiving app and the recipient.

If you choose to send the package to the developer, we receive the image and included information, and may use the contribution to develop or evaluate the identification model. Before sharing, the app tells you that the image and label are offered under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0) licence. Images may include metadata already present in the original file; review the file before sharing if that matters to you.

6Email and support

If you email us, we receive your email address, any name shown by your email service, and the message and attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond to your request. Email is handled by Google's Gmail service under Google's terms. Avoid sending personal or sensitive research information unless it is needed for your request.

7Third-party components

The app includes Google Play Billing, but the current free version does not initiate a billing connection or query products or purchases. Google Play Billing's release notes describe technical logging of billing API outcomes and service-connection issues. We do not receive those logs. The app's current version does not invoke the billing APIs that would produce such activity.

The app uses ONNX Runtime to run the bundled identification model locally; its optional telemetry initializer is removed from the app package. FFmpegKit is used for local video decoding. Syntenomica does not use its own analytics or advertising SDK.

8Retention and deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you delete it in the app, clear the app's data in Android settings, or uninstall the app. You must delete original files and exported or shared copies separately from their own locations.

If you choose to send an image contribution or email, the copy you send is retained in the receiving service or mailbox. A contribution may be used in model development under the CC BY 4.0 licence. Uninstalling the app does not remove information already sent to another person or service.

9Your rights

Where we handle personal data about you, you may contact us to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, or to object to processing, under applicable data-protection law. We cannot access or delete data that exists only in your device storage. You may also complain to the Hungarian National Authority for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (NAIH) or your local supervisory authority.

10Children and changes

Syntenomica is intended for adult researchers and enthusiasts and is not directed at children. We update this page if the app's data practices change. If paid features or new network services are added in a future version, this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration will be updated before that version is released.
