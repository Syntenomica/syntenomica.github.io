Syntenomica Privacy Policy

Syntenomica is a microscopy research app. It processes selected images and videos on your phone. This policy explains local processing, optional file sharing and support requests for the free 0.11.0 test release.

  • Last updated 24 November 2026
  • Applies to Free Android test release 0.11.0 (builds 30 and 31)
  • Contact syntenomica@gmail.com

For version 0.8.6 (25), see the previous policy.

1Who we are

Syntenomica is published by Sági Dániel (Dániel Sági), an independent developer in Hungary. The publisher is the controller for personal data it receives directly, such as support emails and their attachments. Support and privacy contact: syntenomica@gmail.com.

2Data on your device

The app stores private copies of the microscope images and videos you select, projects, sample information, instrument and objective profiles, notes, calibration settings, file fingerprints, previews, and computed results in its private storage on your device. These may include focus composites, relief maps and measurements. The app does not automatically upload these files to a developer server.

Automatic Android backup and device-to-device transfer of the app's private data are disabled. Original recordings remain in the location from which you selected them.

3Processing and permissions

Image and video processing, focus stacking, relief computation and manual measurement run on your device. The 0.11.0 Play builds 30 and 31 exclude the experimental organism classifier and its model. You select files through Android’s system file picker. The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts or location permissions. Imports, analysis tools and STL export are free. Purchase APIs are not called.

4Exporting and sharing

You start an export or share action and choose where it goes using Android's file picker or share sheet. Depending on the action, exported files can contain images or videos, project and sample information, calibration settings, measurements and processing metadata. The receiving app, service or person handles the copy under its own terms. Uninstalling Syntenomica does not delete copies you have exported or shared.

Temporary files created for sharing are kept in the app's private cache and are cleared by the app or Android over time.

5Image contributions

The experimental identification and training-image contribution feature is unavailable in Play builds 30 and 31. Ordinary user-initiated exports and sharing remain available. Earlier preview builds may include the contribution feature; files already voluntarily sent to the publisher are handled under the policy applicable when they were sent.

6Email and support

If you email us, we receive your email address, any name shown by your email service, and the message and attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond to your request. If you volunteer for a closed test, we use the contact and Google account email address you provide to arrange test access and communicate about your feedback. Do not post your Google account email publicly. Email is handled by Google's Gmail service under Google's terms. Avoid sending personal or sensitive research information unless it is needed for your request.

7Third-party components

Purchases are disabled in this free test release. The app does not initialize its Google Play Billing client, query prices or purchases, start a purchase flow, or acknowledge purchases.

The ONNX Runtime library remains packaged but the classifier is disabled and no model is bundled. Its telemetry initializer is removed. FFmpegKit decodes videos locally. Syntenomica uses FFmpegKit and FFmpeg libraries under LGPL version 3 or later. Their unmodified upstream source archives and build scripts are available in the third-party source distribution; the LGPL license applies to those components. Syntenomica has no publisher-operated analytics or advertising service. Google Play distribution is subject to Google’s Privacy Policy.

8Retention and deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you delete it in the app, clear the app's data in Android settings, or uninstall the app. You must delete original files and exported or shared copies separately from their own locations.

If you email the publisher, your message and any attachments are retained in the receiving mailbox. For training images sent voluntarily from earlier preview builds, see Image contributions. Uninstalling the app does not remove information already sent to another person or service.

9Your rights

Where we handle personal data about you, you may contact us to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, or to object to processing, under applicable data-protection law. We cannot access or delete data that exists only in your device storage. You may also complain to the Hungarian National Authority for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (NAIH) or your local supervisory authority.

10Children and changes

Syntenomica is intended for adult researchers and enthusiasts and is not directed at children. We update this page if the app's data practices change. The privacy policy and Google Play Data safety declaration must reflect the data practices of the version being distributed.
