Syntenomica Privacy Policy

Syntenomica is an Android app for microscopy research. It analyses your images and videos on your phone. This policy explains what the app does with your data, what leaves your device and when, and how to contact us.

1Who we are

Syntenomica is developed by Dániel Sági, an independent developer based in Hungary. In this policy, “we” and “us” mean the developer.

For the personal data we handle ourselves, described in sections 4, 6 and 7, we are the data controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). You can reach us at syntenomica@gmail.com.

2Data stored on your device

Syntenomica keeps the following in its private app storage on your phone:

  • private copies of the images and videos you import;
  • research projects, instrument and objective profiles, calibration settings, sample details, notes and other information you enter;
  • results the app computes, such as previews, focus composites, relief (depth) maps, measurements and identification results;
  • SHA-256 fingerprints of imported and derived files, used to check that a file has not changed since import;
  • your daily import count and a saved copy of your Premium status.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. Only you decide whether to export or share any of it (sections 5 and 6).

3How the app processes data

All analysis runs on your phone. This includes frame extraction, focus stacking, relief computation and the experimental organism identification, which uses a model bundled with the app.

Besides an internal permission that Android adds for the app’s own components, Syntenomica uses two permissions:

  • Internet access, used only to communicate with Google Play for purchases;
  • Google Play Billing, used to show offers and complete purchases.

The app has no access to your camera, microphone, location or contacts. You choose files with Android’s system file picker, and the app can open only the files you pick.

Imported originals are kept unchanged. If a file already contains embedded metadata, such as a location recorded by a camera, that metadata stays in the file. Syntenomica does not read or use location data, but an original that you export or share carries its metadata with it.

4Google Play purchases

Premium is sold through Google Play. Google Play handles offers, payment and refunds, and processes your payment information under the Google Privacy Policy. Syntenomica never receives your card or bank details.

To find out which purchases are active, the app asks Google Play on your device and saves the result locally. It confirms new purchases to Google Play, as Google requires. We do not run a purchase server of our own.

Separately from the app, Google Play gives us transaction records in the Play Console, which we need for accounting, tax and refunds, for example the order number, product, price, date and country. These records do not come from Syntenomica on your device. We use them to meet our legal obligations (GDPR Art. 6(1)(c)) and keep them for as long as accounting and tax law requires.

5Exporting and sharing

Nothing leaves the app unless you start it. You can:

  • save a Video Trace JSON record, a result package or relief data to a location you choose with Android’s file picker;
  • share a discovery card, a relief view or a 3D video through Android’s share menu.

Exports can include images, measurements and the project or sample information you entered. The app, service or person you send them to handles them under its own terms. Temporary copies made for sharing are kept in the app’s private cache and cleared over time.

6Contributing images

In the identification view you can bundle an image with the label you believe is correct and send it to us through Android’s share menu, for example by email. Nothing is sent automatically, and you choose the app and the recipient. A contribution package contains:

  • the image file, unchanged, including any metadata it already contains;
  • the label you chose and the identification results the app showed;
  • technical details such as the file name, image size, SHA-256 fingerprint, the app and model version, and when the package was made.

You license the image and label under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0), as the app states before you send it. We use contributions to train and test Syntenomica’s identification model, and they remain part of its training data.

Specimen images are usually not personal data, but a file can carry personal metadata, such as a location recorded by a camera. Check the file before you send it if that matters to you.

If you send a contribution by email, we also receive your email address; see section 7.

7Contacting us by email

When you email us, we receive your email address, your name if your email app includes it, and what you write or attach. We use this only to answer you and to handle your request, such as a contribution, a bug report or a privacy request. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in responding to you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

Correspondence stays in our mailbox, which is a Gmail account, so Google also stores these messages under its own terms. Under the GDPR you can ask us to delete them (section 12).

8Components from other companies

Syntenomica includes the following software components:

  • Google Play Billing Library (Google) connects the app with Google Play for purchases and exchanges purchase information with Google Play to do so. Google’s release notes say the library also logs whether billing calls succeed and any connection problems, and that this information is used to improve the library and to help with errors. We do not receive these logs. If the library sends them off your device, they go to Google, which handles them under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • ONNX Runtime (Microsoft) runs the identification model on your device. It includes an optional usage-reporting feature. Syntenomica turns this feature off, so the app sends no usage or diagnostic data to Microsoft.
  • FFmpegKit (open source) decodes, on your device, video formats that Android cannot open by itself.

9What we do not do

Syntenomica has no user accounts, advertising or tracking, it does not use the Android advertising ID, and we run no analytics of our own. Google’s billing component may send its own technical logs to Google (section 8). We do not sell or rent personal data, and we do not share it with anyone for advertising.

10Security

  • Your data stays in Syntenomica’s private app storage, which Android keeps separate from other apps.
  • Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for Syntenomica’s data, so the app’s data is not copied to a backup service.
  • Purchase communication runs through Google Play’s own encrypted connection.
  • When you share a file, Android gives the receiving app access to that file only.

Keeping your phone locked and up to date also protects the research data stored on it.

11Keeping and deleting data

  • Data on your device stays there until you delete it. Deleting a focus sweep removes its previews and composite. To remove everything, clear Syntenomica’s storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. Neither can be undone, so keep separate copies of your original research files.
  • Exported or shared copies stay wherever you saved or sent them, and you delete them there.
  • Contributions remain part of the training data for the identification model.
  • Emails stay in our mailbox. Under the GDPR you can ask us to delete them (section 12).
  • Transaction records are kept as long as accounting and tax law requires.

12Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us for access to the personal data we hold about you, and for its correction, deletion, restriction or transfer. You can also object to its processing. Email syntenomica@gmail.com.

Your research data is stored only on your device, so we cannot see or delete it for you. You can delete it yourself as described in section 11.

You can also lodge a complaint with the Hungarian data protection authority, the Nemzeti Adatvédelmi és Információszabadság Hatóság (NAIH), or with the data protection authority in your own country.

13Children

Syntenomica is a research tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly receive personal data from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us.
